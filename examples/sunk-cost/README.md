## Meaning and limits

Only irrecoverable past costs are sunk. Future incremental costs/benefits, recoverable value, obligations, and risk still matter. The ticket/resale numbers are explicit teaching assumptions. Unloading a bag does not cancel a contract or recover money. E05 rewrites one selected paragraph; every other HTML byte and the image hash were checked unchanged.
